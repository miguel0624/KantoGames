# KantoGames
test repository for the ano rolbox games

Description:
Welcome to KantoGames, an intense, competitive Roblox experience that brings traditional Filipino street games (Laro ng Lahi) into a dynamic virtual arena! Step into nostalgia with iconic street classics like Tumbang Preso, SekyuBase, Patintero, and more—reimagined for fast-paced multiplayer gameplay.

In KantoGames, simple childhood rules meet modern battle mechanics. Every match features a universal Power-Up System: players can unlock, equip, and strategically trigger abilities—such as speed boosts, shields, teleports, invisibility, and knockback bursts—to turn the tide of any game. Whether you're dodging guards in Patintero or charging the base in SekyuBase, mastering your abilities and coordinating with your team is the key to dominating the street!

Game Modes & Descriptions
Tumbang Preso:
Throwers take turns throwing flip-flops (tsinelas) to knock down a guarded tin can (lata). When the can goes down, throwers rush into the zone to retrieve their slippers and make it back safely before the "Preso" rights the can and tags them out.

SekyuBase (Agawan Base):
A high-stakes team strategy game where two sides fight for territory. Teams venture out from their base to tag enemy players, capture prisoners, and launch daring stealth runs to touch and capture the opponent's base.

Patintero:
The ultimate test of agility and timing. The attacking team tries to cross a grid of lines guarded by the defending team without getting touched. One wrong move or missed line guard can wipe out an entire run!

Tagutaguan (Hide and Seek):
The "Taya" (seeker) counts at the home base while hiders blend into the map environment. Once spotted, hiders must use mobility, trickery, and shortcuts to race back to the base and tap out safely before being caught.

Sipa:
A thrilling game of coordination and aerial control. Players must kick a weighted shuttlecock (sipa) into the air, keeping it off the ground while performing high-combo tricks or volleying it back and forth over a central boundary.

Luksong Baka:
A fast-paced leapfrogging game where players take turns jumping over the "Baka" (it). As the "Baka" grows taller and changes stances each round, timing, height, and jump placement become critical to avoiding a wipeout.

Langit-Lupa:
An adrenaline-fueled game of tag where elevated surfaces (Langit) provide temporary safety from the "Taya" on the ground (Lupa). Staying elevated too long triggers a cooldown, forcing players back into the danger zone!
